# Europe 2025

From Apple Notes (`Norway 2025` through `Germany 2025`, plus `Summer 2025`). Dates and places only — no booking codes, lockboxes, or confirmation numbers.

**10 June – 29 August 2025.** Oslo to Berlin, then home via JFK and Providence.

## Norway · 10–17 June

- **10 Jun** Arrive Oslo. Airport train into town.
- **11 Jun** Oslo: Akershus, Aker Brygge, Frogner Park, National Museum, Opera House roof.
- **12 Jun** Train Oslo → Bergen (~7h).
- **13 Jun** Fløibanen, Fløyen hike, Bryggen, Hanseatic Museum.
- **14 Jun** Norway in a Nutshell, self-guided: Bergen → Voss train, bus to Gudvangen, ferry to Flåm, Flåm railway back to Bergen.
- **15 Jun** Gamlehaugen gardens, Fantoft Stave Church.
- **16 Jun** Bergenhus Fortress, Ulriken.
- **17 Jun** Fly Bergen → Gdańsk.

## Poland · 17 June – 3 July

- **17–19 Jun** Gdańsk old town, Museum of the Second World War, city walks.
- **20 Jun** Malbork Castle day trip.
- **21 Jun** Gdańsk / Gdynia.
- **22 Jun** Car from Gdańsk: Westerplatte, Toruń, Łódź, Warsaw.
- **23–25 Jun** Warsaw: Łazienki Park, Wilanów, old town, Royal Castle, Palace of Culture.
- **26–28 Jun** Poznań; **28 Jun** day trip to Gniezno (cathedral, locomotive depot, lake playgrounds).
- **29 Jun** Train via Wrocław (market square, dwarves, Cathedral Island) to Kraków.
- **30 Jun – 2 Jul** Kraków: Schindler’s Factory, ghetto memorial, Wawel, Rynek Underground, Zakrzówek. Messers join one night.
- **3 Jul** Fly Kraków → Vienna.

## Austria · 3–10 July

- Two cars from Vienna Airport. Base in **Wels**.
- Days around Gmunden / the lakes, Linz, Salzburg, and Berchtesgaden.
- **10 Jul** Return cars at VIE; onward to Bratislava.

## Slovakia · 10–13 July

- **10–11 Jul** Bratislava old town, Blue Church, castle.
- **12 Jul** Devín Castle; swim in a fountain back in town.
- **13 Jul** Train Bratislava → Budapest.

## Hungary · 13–21 July

- **13–17 Jul** Budapest: Dagály pools, Children’s Railway to Normafa and Elizabeth Lookout, Buda Castle, Margaret Island, City Park (Városliget).
- **18–19 Jul** Overnight in Pécs (Cella Septichora, Christian necropolis).
- **20 Jul** Margaret Island bike cart; Szimpla Kert market.
- **21 Jul** Fly Budapest → Brașov.

## Romania · 21 July – 1 August

- **21–24 Jul** Brașov: Seven Ladders Canyon, bear sanctuary, Bran Castle, Peleș (Sinaia), Râșnov, Council Square, Black Church, Tâmpa.
- **25–26 Jul** Drive via Hărman, Prejmer, Viscri fortified churches to Sighișoara; medieval festival.
- **27–31 Jul** Sibiu: old town, Bridge of Lies, ASTRA, Râpa Roșie, Alba Iulia, zoo; **31 Jul** Turda Gorge and salt mine.
- **1 Aug** Fly Sibiu → Vienna.

## Czechia · 1–12 August

- **1 Aug** Vienna morning, then train to Brno.
- **1–2 Aug** Brno: Špilberk, Freedom Square, St. Peter and Paul, Zelný trh.
- **3 Aug** Train Brno → Prague, pick up a car, drive to Český Krumlov (kids to camp).
- **4 Aug** Krumlov Castle, Hluboká Castle, Kutná Hora (Sedlec Ossuary, St. Barbara’s).
- **5–6 Aug** Bohemian Paradise: Prachov Rocks, Trosky, Hrubá Skála, Valdštejn.
- **7 Aug** Karlovy Vary: Moser, colonnades, Diana tower.
- **8 Aug** Pilsner Urquell tour in Plzeň.
- **9–11 Aug** Prague: kids back from camp at Opatov, Vyšehrad boat, meet Zachary Harrod.
- **12 Aug** Fly Prague → Bergamo.

## Italy · 12–19 August

- **12–13 Aug** Bergamo: Città Alta, Santa Maria Maggiore, San Vigilio, Mazzini pizza.
- **14–18 Aug** Dolomites from Perarolo di Cadore: Cinque Torri / Rifugio Nuvolau, Cortina, Dobbiaco & Sesto / Tre Cime.
- **19 Aug** Fly Bergamo → Charleroi (Brussels South).

## Belgium, Netherlands, Germany · 19–29 August

- **19–22 Aug** Liège: Citadel, Montagne de Bueren, Curtius, Guillemins station, Fort de la Chartreuse, La Boverie.
- **21 Aug** Aachen (cathedral, printen) and Monschau.
- **23 Aug** Valkenburg (Mergelrijk, toboggan, castle) and Maastricht.
- **24 Aug** Tongeren: Gallo-Roman Museum, basilica, begijnhof, Ambiorix.
- **25 Aug** Leuven morning, then European Sleeper Bruxelles-Midi → Berlin Ostbahnhof overnight.
- **26–28 Aug** Berlin: East Side Gallery, playgrounds, Museum Island (Pergamon Panorama, Alte Nationalgalerie, Altes Museum, Neues Museum / Nefertiti, PETRI).
- **29 Aug** Norse Berlin → JFK; Hertz; Springfield Museums **30 Aug**; home to Jacksonville **1 Sep**.
